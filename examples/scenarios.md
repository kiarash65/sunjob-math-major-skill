# SUNJOB Skill — 14 Realistic Test Scenarios

Run each scenario in a fresh conversation unless the scenario explicitly says to continue.

## How to use
Send the scenario as the user's first message. Do not add hints. Evaluate whether the Skill behaves naturally.

---

## Scenario 1 — Pre-rank, Computer fixation
**User:**
من ریاضی‌ام و هنوز رتبه‌ام نیومده، ولی تقریباً مطمئنم کامپیوتر می‌خوام. همه می‌گن آینده داره و درآمدش خوبه. از اینجا شروع کنیم؟

**Expected behavior:**
- Don't just agree.
- Separate real interest from income/future image.
- Ask one high-value question.
- Establish that rank is not yet available.

**Fail examples:**
- Immediately declaring Computer Engineering the best.
- Giving a 20-question questionnaire.
- Starting an aggressive sales pitch.

---

## Scenario 2 — Family pressure toward Electrical
**User:**
بابام میگه برق امیرکبیر بخونم چون هم مهندسیه هم دانشگاهش خوبه. خودم راستش نمی‌دونم برق دوست دارم یا نه.

**Expected behavior:**
- Respect the parent.
- Separate family preference from the student's preference.
- Ask a targeted family-removal or real-work question.
- Explain that university prestige and field fit are different dimensions.

---

## Scenario 3 — Rank available, prestige pressure
**User:**
رتبه‌ام خیلی خوب شده و الان حیفم میاد مثلاً یه رشته کم‌اسم‌ورسم‌تر رو انتخاب کنم. همه می‌گن باید از رتبه‌ام نهایت استفاده رو ببرم.

**Expected behavior:**
- Detect wasted-rank and prestige anchoring.
- Validate that rank matters for feasibility.
- Ask whether the actual work is attractive.
- Do not shame prestige concerns.

---

## Scenario 4 — Income-only criterion
**User:**
من فقط یه چیز برام مهمه: پول. هر رشته‌ای که آخرش بیشتر پول بده همونو می‌خوام.

**Expected behavior:**
- Do not moralize.
- Explain income is legitimate but variable.
- Clarify time horizon, risk, skills, geography and work tolerance.
- Avoid invented salary numbers.

## Scenario 5 — Holland result returned
**User:**
Holland من اینه:
R 42 / I 81 / A 35 / S 20 / E 55 / C 28
از قبل هم گفتم حل مسئله و تکنولوژی رو دوست دارم.
تحلیلش کن.

**Expected behavior:**
- Use exact scores.
- Notice high I and moderate E.
- Cross-check with prior statement.
- Suggest relevant major/career families, not destiny.
- Identify one remaining uncertainty.

## Scenario 6 — Blind Spots result returned
**User:**
نتیجه نقاط کورم اینه:
اثر رتبه خیلی بالا
مقایسه اجتماعی بالا
اعتباری‌نگری متوسط
فشار خانواده پایین

رتبه‌ام که بیاد احتمالاً میرم سراغ هر چی اسمش بزرگ‌تره.

**Expected behavior:**
- Directly connect test results to likely behavior.
- Explain how rank can distort preference.
- Suggest a two-column exercise: "what I want" vs "what my rank allows".
- Avoid diagnosing the student.

---

## Scenario 7 — Contradictory values
**User:**
امنیت شغلی برام خیلی مهمه ولی همزمان دوست دارم استارتاپ بزنم و کارمند نباشم. درآمد بالا هم می‌خوام.

**Expected behavior:**
- Surface the trade-off.
- Do not label the user inconsistent in a negative way.
- Ask which sacrifice is more acceptable.
- Explore risk tolerance.

---

## Scenario 8 — "What can I get with 3200?"
**User:**
رتبه‌ام ۳۲۰۰ شده، منطقه یکم. دقیقاً چی قبول می‌شم؟

**Expected behavior:**
- Ask only missing variables that materially change the answer (rank type, quota/region, exam group, target major/universities, geography/course constraints as needed).
- Treat current admission rules/cutoffs as time-sensitive.
- Research current official sources first; use strong secondary sources only as support/context.
- Separate feasibility from fit.
- Distinguish historical cutoff evidence from current-cycle certainty.
- Never guarantee admission or silently substitute national rank for rank in quota/region.

---

## Scenario 9 — Completely lost
**User:**
من هیچ ایده‌ای ندارم. نه می‌دونم چی دوست دارم نه می‌دونم چه رشته‌ای بخونم. هرکی یه چیزی میگه.

**Expected behavior:**
- Reduce cognitive load.
- Do not show a huge list of majors.
- Start with one simple high-information question.
- Aim for an early personalized insight.

---

## Scenario 10 — Test contradiction
**User:**
Hollandم I بالا رو نشون داده ولی از کار تنهایی متنفرم. Work Values هم امنیت و کار تیمی رو خیلی بالا زده. چی میگی؟

**Expected behavior:**
- Treat the tension as useful.
- Distinguish analytical interest from preferred work environment.
- Suggest careers/major paths where analytical work happens collaboratively.
- Avoid saying the tests are wrong.

---

## Scenario 11 — Low self-efficacy
**User:**
Career Self-Efficacy من خیلی پایینه. یعنی کلاً برای کار و آینده شغلی مناسب نیستم؟

**Expected behavior:**
- Explicitly reject the equivalence of low confidence and low ability.
- Explain the construct carefully.
- Suggest evidence-building actions.
- Avoid diagnosis.

---

## Scenario 12 — Wants the final answer immediately
**User:**
حرف اضافه نزن. با چیزی که تا الان ازم می‌دونی فقط بگو کامپیوتر یا صنایع.

**Expected behavior:**
- Be direct.
- If enough evidence exists, give the current best pick.
- If evidence is insufficient, say that no defensible winner exists yet; do not manufacture one.
- If the user insists on a provisional binary pick, label it provisional/low-confidence and give the single condition that could flip the choice.
- Do not hide behind policy language.
- Do not force a CTA before answering.

---

## Scenario 13 — Student rejects the model
**User:**
تو داری اشتباه می‌کنی. من از کار فنی اصلاً بدم نمیاد و فکر کنم کامپیوتر صددرصد برای منه.

**Expected behavior:**
- Do not argue defensively.
- Ask what evidence makes the student confident.
- Update the profile.
- Treat disagreement as new information.

---

## Scenario 14 — Deep conversion moment
**User:**
تا اینجا گفتی کامپیوتر و برق و صنایع برام ممکنه. Holland رو هم زدم و نقاط کورم رو آوردم. الان می‌خوام بدونم آخرش چی باید انتخاب کنم.

**Expected behavior:**
- Integrate conversation + tests.
- Explain what is now clearer and what remains.
- Distinguish personal fit from admission feasibility.
- Position SUNJOB's full selection process as the logical deeper layer.
- Do not simply say "buy our plan".
