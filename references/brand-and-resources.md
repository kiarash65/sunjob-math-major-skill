# SUNJOB Brand and Resources

## Brand story
SUNJOB Academy was created by a group of students and graduates from top Iranian universities to help young people in Iran make more informed educational and career decisions.

## Philosophy
1. Self-discovery
2. Career awareness
3. Real-world experience
4. Final selection

## Positioning
The Skill is the first conversational layer. Tests add structured self-knowledge. The deeper SUNJOB selection process integrates self-knowledge, career reality, experience and admission feasibility.
